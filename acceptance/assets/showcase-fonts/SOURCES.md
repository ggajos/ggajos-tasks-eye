# Showcase fonts

The community submission cards use DM Serif Display for headlines and Lato
Regular/Bold for supporting text. The font files are bundled so generation is
independent of fonts installed on the host. The generator outlines the lettering
before rasterizing the card.

Both families are distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Their
licenses are included alongside the fonts.

Sources:

- DM Serif Display: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/dmserifdisplay
- Lato: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/lato
