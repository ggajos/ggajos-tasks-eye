## Choose a day when you can act

When a note's earliest unchecked due date falls on a non-working weekday,
public holiday, or personal time off, its row explains the conflict. Move the
task to a day when you can give it attention, or correct your availability
settings if the day should be available.

The message lists every reason for that date, including named holidays and
personal labels. Conflicts on later-dated tasks are not shown on the row until
they become the note's earliest unchecked due date.
