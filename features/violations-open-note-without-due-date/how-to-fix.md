## How to fix

Add `📅 YYYY-MM-DD` to at least one unchecked task. Choose the day you want
the note to return to your attention:

```md
- [ ] Review the proposal 📅 2026-10-01
```

Replace the example date with your chosen day. A date on a completed task does
not count, and Tasks scheduled or start markers do not satisfy this rule.
Other unchecked tasks can remain undated.
