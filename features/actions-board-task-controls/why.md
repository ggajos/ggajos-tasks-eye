## Act on the next task

Hover over a row or focus its controls to complete the selected task, move its
date, or change its priority. Every action updates the task in the original note.

- **Checkmark:** complete the task through Tasks. Recurring tasks follow Tasks'
  completion behavior.
- **Date controls:** move the due date by the displayed number of calendar
  days. They do not skip weekends, holidays, or personal time off.
- **Priority arrows:** move one step higher or lower. Priority changes ordering
  among notes whose next actions share a date.

Open the note when you need its supporting information or want to edit a
different task. After completing the visible action, choose and date the next
step if work remains.
