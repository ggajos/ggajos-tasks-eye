IDEA: The entire idea is about attention. Some either require attention or not. Tasks eye workflow is trying to distill signal from noice and at the same time ensure that nothing slips out.

Inbox - A temporary holding space (physical or digital) where you capture all incoming tasks, ideas, requests, and information before processing them.
Tasks Eye:
 - This exists.
 - When creating a note which is without due date (attention date), then it is immidiately in inbox, for refinement, awaiting to add due date

Capture - The first step of the GTD workflow: collecting everything that has your attention into a trusted inbox so it is out of your head.
Tasks Eye:
 - Any created note without due date will land in inbox

Clarify - The second step: taking each item from your inbox and deciding exactly what it means and if it is actionable (identifying the very next action).
Tasks Eye:
 - This is creating a task in the note and attaching due date

Organize - The third step: sorting the clarified items into the appropriate lists (e.g., Next Actions, Projects, Waiting For) or filing systems.
Tasks Eye:
 - This is just processing inbox

Reflect / Review - The fourth step: regularly reviewing your lists (typically weekly) to ensure your system is up-to-date, relevant, and trusted.
Tasks Eye:
 - It is good to have a note reflect/review with some checklist and due date when the review should be performed (it can use task recurring)

Engage - The fifth step: simply doing the work by choosing the best action to take right now based on your context, time, energy, and priority.
Tasks Eye:
  - Empty the Focus bucket, you start your day with the focus list, a the goal is to make this list empty.
  - By empty, you can also reschedule (if amount of work for today is done)

Projects - Any outcome you are committed to achieving that requires more than one action step to complete (e.g., "Plan vacation," "Launch website").
Tasks Eye:
  - This is just a note. Any note.

Next Action - The very next physical, visible step that needs to be taken to move a project or task forward.
Tasks Eye:
  - Task with due date (attention date)

Contexts - The tools, location, or people required to complete an action (e.g., @computer, @office, @errands), used to filter tasks based on where you are and what you have available.
Tasks Eye:
  - Context are derived from directory path, so this is 1-1 with folder structure

The 2-Minute Rule - A rule during the "Clarify" stage: if an action takes less than two minutes to complete, do it immediately rather than writing it down.
Tasks Eye:
  - I would suggest to extend this rule and if something take longer
  - The goal of noting is to decrease friction of context switching, so most important part is to capture status of task that can help you switch to new context with little friction possible; so it is more important to capture some information for future self;
  - you can note something that take less than 2 minutes if it's important for future referece, I'm talking about done view, this is the place where you can see what has been done, if this is simple thing you just write it down as done, so it pops there (done require date)

Waiting For - A list used to track tasks, deliverables, or information that you have delegated or are waiting to receive from someone else.
Tasks Eye:
  - There is no different list for that. I flipped the question. If you are waiting for something then you can ask yourself when is it moment when you should ping someone, this is next due date, attention moment. So you can capture waiting as task with due date so it will resurface in Focus or Open when needed. No need for separate list.

Someday / Maybe - A list for ideas, projects, or tasks you might want to do in the future, but are not actively committing to right now.
Tasks Eye:
  - Recommended approach here is to have a single note with someday/maybe which listing all ideas (it can list other notes as well), ideally it should have due date to review this list weekly/monthly as you like

Calendar - A tool used strictly for time-specific or day-specific actions and appointments (the "hard landscape" of your day).
Tasks Eye:
  - This plugin is not replacing calendar.

Reference Material - Information or files that are not actionable but need to be kept for future use or reference, stored in an organized filing system.
Tasks Eye:
  - Reference material is note with status closed

Trash - Items that are not actionable and hold no reference value; things you simply throw away or delete.
Tasks Eye:
  - Trash material is note with status closed or deleted permanently.

Project Support Material - Resources, notes, and plans related to a specific active project, kept separate from action lists but accessible when working on the project.
Tasks Eye:
  - See reference material.

Tickler File - A system (often 43 physical folders) used to hold reminders or physical items that you want to see on a specific future date.
Tasks Eye:
  - This is something that can be implemented in multiple ways (or all those ways at once)
    - Single big file with many tasks, house maintanance tasks for full year splitted by month section, you just need to ensure that single task have due date (so it is next attention date), when done you can select what require attention next
    - Many small files with cycles; So you can have a note with monthly recurring task to check/fix/do something, this will resurface to next month when you check it out, also you can capture some context/notes there if needed 
    - In other words, tickler can be spread across notes as you like; Inbox will ensure that you didn't slip something out.

Higher-Level Horizons of Focus - The different altitudes of perspective in GTD (from daily actions up to life purpose) that help you ensure your daily tasks align with your broader goals and values.
Tasks Eye:
  - Those are just notes with due date tasks to review them.
